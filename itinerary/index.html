Practical notes

TransitSuica on each phone, top up at any station machine or with the wallet app. No JR Pass; the only long-distance rail is Mishima to Kyoto. A Kawaguchiko Red/Green line 2-day bus pass (¥1,500) for Oct 24 to 25.
LuggageOne takkyubin send from a Tokyo convenience store on Oct 23 to the Kyoto hotel. Overnight bag only for Fuji. Kyoto to Nara to Osaka is carried; Nara uses station lockers for four hours.
RoomsTokyo is one apartment for four. Fuji is a three-bedroom villa. Kyoto and Osaka are two doubles each. Decide pairings once.
WeatherTokyo 14 to 20°C, Kyoto and Osaka similar. Kawaguchiko at dawn about 5°C, Nikko's lake similar. One warm layer and a light rain shell each; October has a typhoon tail some years, check the week before.
ClosuresImperial East Gardens closed Mon and Fri (you go Tue). Aoniyoshi rests on Thursdays (you ride Tue). Most Tokyo museums close Mon. Todai-ji closes 17:00 in October.
CashOmoide Yokocho, Golden Gai, Jindaiji soba, Kawaguchiko hoto shops, Nishiki stalls, Nara crackers, Kuromon and Dotonbori counters are cash-first. 7-Eleven ATMs take foreign cards; ¥20,000 a head in reserve is enough.
Fuji oddsLate October is statistically the clearest month. Even so, mornings win: Chureito on the 24th and the lakeshore on the 25th are the two shots. If both are clouded, Chureito is still worth it for the pagoda and maples.
